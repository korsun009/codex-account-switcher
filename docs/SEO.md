## Content Rules

- Keep keywords in natural sentences, not repeated keyword lists only.
- Put download links near the top of each language page.
- Keep safety statements explicit: the app switches only `auth.json` and does not upload tokens.
- Use the same product name across all languages: `Codex Account Switcher`.
- Do not promise bypassing limits, free usage, or account sharing.
